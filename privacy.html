Privacy Policy

Vectorburst: Arrow Puzzle for iPhone · Last updated 27 October 2026

The short version

  • There are no accounts. We never ask for your name, email address, phone number or contacts.
  • Your game progress is saved on your iPhone.
  • Ads are shown by Google AdMob, starting after the Level 6 win. Tracking happens only if you allow it in Apple's prompt.
  • Usage statistics are off unless you say yes, and you can switch them off again in Settings.
  • Players whose age range says they're under 14 see no ads and send no statistics, so we collect nothing from them.

Who we are

Vectorburst is made by an independent developer based in Albania: the seller named on the app's App Store page ("we", "us"), who is responsible for your personal information. For any question about privacy, email vectorburst.help@gmail.com.

What stays on your iPhone

Your progress, shards, unlocks, streaks, settings and the age range you shared (if any) are saved in the app's own storage on your device. We don't run a server that stores them. They are included in iCloud and computer backups of your iPhone, and they are deleted when you delete the app. One small exception: the date the Starter Pack first went on sale is kept in your iPhone's keychain, which iOS keeps even after the app is deleted, so that reinstalling doesn't restart the offer's 7 days. It contains nothing else.

Purchases

Apple handles all payments. We never see your name, your Apple Account or your payment details. Apple tells the app what you bought so the app can give you the item and restore it later.

Advertising by Google AdMob

The game shows ads from Google AdMob: rewarded ads that you choose to watch for a reward, and from Level 12 on, ads between levels, which the Remove Ads purchase turns off. Ads start only after the Level 6 win, after the age question (on iPhones that support it) and Google's consent check. To show and measure ads and to prevent fraud, Google's software in the app may collect:

  • your IP address, which is used to estimate your general location (such as your country or city, never your precise location);
  • information about the ads you see and tap, and about how you use the app, such as when it opens;
  • information about your device, such as its model, iOS version and crash or performance data;
  • device identifiers: Apple's identifier for this developer's apps on your iPhone and, only if you allow tracking, your device's advertising identifier.

Google may share some of this information with advertisers and other ad companies that compete to show an ad. In the European Economic Area, the UK and Switzerland, Google's consent form names those companies before any ads load (for the players it asks; see “Younger players and age”).

The app asks Google for ads that suit all ages. If you see one that doesn't, please report it and we'll look for it and block it.

Google's use of this data is described in the Google Privacy Policy, in How Google uses information from sites or apps that use its services and in its data retention policy. If you have a Google Account, you can also control how Google personalizes ads in My Ad Center.

Your choices about ads

  • Tracking. From Level 12, the app explains what tracking means and then shows Apple's standard prompt, which asks whether Vectorburst may track your activity across other companies' apps and websites. If you choose "Ask App Not to Track", Google doesn't receive your advertising identifier and the ads you see are less personalized. You can change this at any time in iOS Settings › Privacy & Security › Tracking. Players whose age range is under 18 are never asked.
  • Consent. In the European Economic Area, the UK and Switzerland, Google's consent form asks for your choices before any ads load (players whose age range is under 16 aren't asked; see below). In US states with privacy laws, you can opt out of the sale or sharing of your personal information. Where these apply, you can change your choices at any time in Vectorburst Settings › Privacy choices.
  • Remove Ads. This purchase turns off the ads between levels.

Younger players and age

Vectorburst is a game for a general audience. It is not made for children and is not in the Kids category on the App Store. The app uses Apple's Declared Age Range feature, which shares only an age range (never a birth date) when the player or a parent allows it. The app asks for it after the Level 6 win; where Apple says the law requires apps to check age (such as in Texas), it asks when the game first opens, and again once a year. The range is kept with your progress on your iPhone; Google receives only the ad settings it leads to.

  • Under 18: the app never shows the tracking prompt, pop-up purchase offers or offers of extra taps before boss levels, and its reminders and messages never warn about losing a streak. It asks Google for ads that aren't personalized, with Google's protections for teens. If usage statistics were on, they are switched off as soon as the app learns the range.
  • Under 16: Google is also told that the player is under the age of consent, so in Europe its consent form doesn't ask them for consent.
  • Under 14: the app shows no ads at all and sends no usage statistics, so we collect no personal information from these players. Apple's own services, such as Game Center, work as Apple describes in its privacy policy.
  • No age shared (an iPhone before iOS 26, or the player chose not to share): ads work as described above but, as for players under 18, there are no pop-up purchase offers (the Starter Pack and the Shard Vault are in the Shop instead), no offers of extra taps before boss levels and no warnings about losing a streak.

Nothing is sent to Google or TelemetryDeck before the Level 6 win and the age question (on iPhones that support it): ads start only after both, and usage statistics only if you then say yes. If you believe a child under 13 has sent us personal information, for example by email, contact us and we will delete it.

Usage statistics (only if you agree)

After the Level 6 win and the age question, the game asks once whether you want to share usage statistics. They stay off unless you say yes, and from then on you can switch them on or off at any time in Vectorburst Settings › Share usage statistics. Players whose age range is under 18 aren't asked (from 14 they can switch them on in Settings), and players under 14 can't switch them on.

If you say yes, the app sends usage events to TelemetryDeck, an analytics service based in Germany, to show us which levels are too hard and which features people use. Events say things like "Level 12 finished in 4 taps", "the Shop was opened" or which product was bought. TelemetryDeck also receives your device model, iOS and app version, screen size, language, region and time-zone settings, some accessibility settings (such as text size and Reduce Motion), and how often and how long you play. Each event carries a scrambled (salted and hashed) version of Apple's identifier for this developer's apps on your iPhone, so we can count players without knowing who they are. Statistics never contain your name, email address, contacts, precise location or anything you type, and TelemetryDeck says it never stores IP addresses. It keeps raw events for several years: it says it expects to delete them after 7 to 10 years.

Game Center

If you are signed in to Game Center, the game sends your scores and achievements to Apple's Game Center so they appear on leaderboards and in Game Center challenges with your friends. Other players may see your Game Center nickname and scores. If you are already signed in to Game Center on your device, the game connects to it when it opens; if not, it offers the Game Center sign-in the first time you open the Daily Burst after the Level 6 win. Apple keeps Game Center data under its Privacy Policy; deleting the app doesn't delete it, and you can manage it in your iPhone's Game Center settings.

Notifications and clips

If you turn on the daily reminder, it is scheduled on your iPhone; no notification server is involved. Clips of your chains are recorded on your iPhone with Apple's ReplayKit and are shared only if you choose to share them.

This website

This website is hosted by GitHub Pages. GitHub logs visitors' IP addresses for security, as GitHub explains. The site has no cookies, no analytics and no ads. Challenge links that players share contain only a level number and a number of taps.

Other things to know

  • If you share analytics with app developers in your iPhone's settings, Apple gives us combined crash and usage reports through App Store Connect. They don't identify you.
  • If you email us, we receive your email address and message in a Gmail account (Google). We use them only to answer you, and delete them once they're no longer needed for that.
  • We don't collect your name, phone number, contacts, photos, precise location, health or financial information, or anything you type in the game.

Who receives data

Data from the app goes to Google (ads and consent; Google may share some of it with advertisers and other ad companies, as described above), to TelemetryDeck (usage statistics, only if you agree) and to Apple (purchases, Game Center and the reports above). These companies are bound by their terms with us, and by data protection law, to protect the data they receive at least as well as this policy describes.

Why we use data (Europe, the UK and Switzerland)

  • Ads: where the law requires it, your consent, which you give or refuse in Google's consent form and can change at any time in Vectorburst Settings › Privacy choices. Otherwise, our legitimate interest in keeping the game free with ads.
  • Usage statistics: your consent, which you can withdraw at any time in Settings.
  • Age range: to meet our legal duties where apps must check age, and our legitimate interest in protecting younger players.
  • Purchases: to give you what you bought.
  • Emails you send us: to answer you.

You never have to share any of this to play: the game plays the same if you refuse tracking or keep statistics off, and if you don't share your age the only differences are the ones described above (pop-up offers stay in the Shop, and there are no extra-tap offers before boss levels and no streak warnings).

Where data goes

We are based in Albania. TelemetryDeck GmbH is in Germany. Google and Apple may process data in the United States and other countries. For data from Europe, the UK and Switzerland, Google relies on the EU–US Data Privacy Framework (with its UK and Swiss extensions) and on the European Commission's standard contractual clauses.

How long data is kept, and your rights

We don't keep personal information on our own servers. Statistics at TelemetryDeck and data at Google are kept as described above. Depending on where you live, you can ask us what personal information we have about you, and ask us to correct it, delete it, give you a copy, or stop or limit using it. Where we rely on your consent, you can withdraw it at any time. Using any of these rights never changes how the game treats you.

In practice, most of this is in your hands: switch statistics off in Settings, change your ad choices in Settings, and delete the app to delete your progress. For anything else, including a request under the GDPR, Canadian law (including Quebec's), Albanian law or US state privacy laws, email us and we will reply within 30 days. We may ask you to reply from the same email address, or for a detail only you would know, to confirm a request is yours. In Canada, including Quebec, the person in charge of protecting personal information is the owner of Vectorburst, at the same email address. You also have the right to complain to your local data protection authority.

US state privacy laws

In the last 12 months the app has collected, through Google's software: identifiers (IP address and device identifiers), internet activity (how you use the app and the ads you see), approximate location from your IP address and, if you allow tracking, inferences about your interests that Google uses for personalized ads; and, only if you agree to usage statistics, through TelemetryDeck: a scrambled device identifier, device details and settings, purchase history and how you use the app. It comes from your device and is used for the purposes in this policy. Personalized ads through Google may count as "sharing" for cross-context behavioral advertising, or as a "sale", under some state laws; you can opt out in Vectorburst Settings › Privacy choices or by choosing "Ask App Not to Track". We don't knowingly sell or share the personal information of consumers under 16: players whose age range is under 16 never get personalized ads. We don't collect sensitive personal information. You can use an authorized agent to make a request, with your signed permission. If we decline a request, you can appeal by replying to our answer with "Appeal", and we will answer within 45 days; if you're still not satisfied, you can contact your state's attorney general.
