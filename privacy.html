Privacy Policy

Vectorburst: Arrow Puzzle for iPhone · Last updated 27 October 2026

The short version

  • There are no accounts. We never ask for your name, email address, phone number or contacts.
  • Your game progress is saved on your iPhone.
  • Ads are shown by Google AdMob. Tracking happens only if you allow it in Apple's prompt.
  • Anonymous usage statistics help us improve the game, and you can switch them off in Settings.

Who we are

Vectorburst is made by an independent developer: the seller named on the app's App Store page ("we", "us"), who is responsible for your personal information. For any question about privacy, email vectorburst.help@gmail.com.

What stays on your iPhone

Your progress, shards, unlocks, streaks and settings are saved in the app's own storage on your device. We don't run a server that stores them. They are included in iCloud and computer backups of your iPhone, and they are deleted when you delete the app.

Purchases

Apple handles all payments. We never see your name, your Apple Account or your payment details. Apple tells the app what you bought so the app can give you the item and restore it later.

Advertising by Google AdMob

The game shows ads from Google AdMob: rewarded ads that you choose to watch for a reward, and from Level 12 on, ads between levels, which the Remove Ads purchase turns off. To show and measure ads and to prevent fraud, Google's software in the app may collect:

  • your IP address, which is used to estimate your general location (such as your country or city, never your precise location);
  • information about the ads you see and tap, and about how you use the app, such as when it opens;
  • information about your device, such as its model, iOS version and crash or performance data;
  • identifiers that Google creates for this app, and your device's advertising identifier only if you allow tracking.

The app asks Google for ads that suit all ages. If you see one that doesn't, please report it and we'll block it.

Google's use of this data is described in the Google Privacy Policy and in How Google uses information from sites or apps that use its services. You can see and control how Google personalizes ads in My Ad Center.

Your choices about ads

  • Tracking. From Level 12, just before ads between levels start, the app explains what tracking means and then shows Apple's standard prompt. If you choose "Ask App Not to Track", Google does not receive your advertising identifier and the ads you see are less personalized. You can change this at any time in iOS Settings › Privacy & Security › Tracking.
  • Consent. In the European Economic Area, the UK and Switzerland, Google's consent form asks for your choices before any ads load. In US states with privacy laws, you can opt out of the sale or sharing of your personal information. Where these apply, you can change your choices at any time in Vectorburst Settings › Privacy choices.
  • Remove Ads. This purchase turns off the ads between levels.

Younger players

Vectorburst is a game for a general audience. It is not made for children and is not in the Kids category on the App Store. The app uses Apple's Declared Age Range feature, which shares only an age range (never a birth date) when the player or a parent allows it. If the range is under 16, the app never shows the tracking prompt or pop-up offers, and it asks Google for non-personalized ads only, with Google's protections for teens. If the range is under 13, ad requests are marked as directed at children instead. The age range stays on the device; Google only receives those ad settings.

Players under 13. When the age range shows a player is under 13, usage statistics start switched off, and the only personal information collected from them is technical: the IP address and the app-scoped identifiers that Google receives to show ads. These are used only to run the app: to show ads that aren't personalized and limit how often they appear, to prevent fraud and to keep the app working. Because the ad requests are marked as directed at children, Google may not use them to contact anyone, to build a profile or for personalized advertising. If usage statistics are switched back on in Settings, TelemetryDeck counts them with a hashed identifier that can't identify or contact the player. We keep none of this information ourselves; Google keeps it only as long as its data retention policy allows. If you believe a child has sent us personal information, email us and we will delete it.

Anonymous usage statistics

To learn which levels are too hard and which features people use, the app sends anonymous usage events to TelemetryDeck, a privacy-focused analytics service based in Germany. Events say things like "Level 12 finished in 4 taps", "the Shop was opened" or which product was bought. They never contain your name, email address, contacts, location or anything you type. TelemetryDeck counts players with an anonymous, hashed identifier, so it can't tell who you are. Switch this off at any time in Vectorburst Settings › Share anonymous usage data.

Game Center

If you are signed in to Game Center, the game sends your scores and achievements to Apple's Game Center so they appear on leaderboards and in Game Center challenges with your friends. Other players may see your Game Center nickname and scores. If you are already signed in to Game Center on your device, the game connects to it when it opens; if not, it offers the Game Center sign-in the first time you open the Daily Burst. Apple's Privacy Policy covers Game Center.

Notifications and clips

If you turn on the daily reminder, it is scheduled on your iPhone; no notification server is involved. Clips of your chains are recorded on your iPhone with Apple's ReplayKit and are shared only if you choose to share them.

What we don't collect

We don't collect your name, email address, phone number, contacts, photos, precise location, health or financial information, or anything you type. If you email us, we use your message and email address only to answer you.

The only companies that receive data from the app are the ones named in this policy: Google (ads and consent), TelemetryDeck (anonymous statistics) and Apple (purchases and Game Center). We only work with services that protect your data at least as well as this policy describes.

Why we use data (Europe, the UK and Switzerland)

  • Ads: where the law requires it, your consent, which you give or refuse in Google's consent form and can change at any time in Vectorburst Settings › Privacy choices. Otherwise, our legitimate interest in keeping the game free with ads.
  • Usage statistics: our legitimate interest in finding out which levels are too hard and which features people use. You can object at any time by switching them off in Settings.
  • Purchases: to give you what you bought.
  • Emails you send us: to answer you.

Where data goes

TelemetryDeck GmbH is a company in Germany. Google may process data in the United States and other countries. For data from Europe, the UK and Switzerland, Google relies on the EU–US Data Privacy Framework (with its UK and Swiss extensions) and on the European Commission's standard contractual clauses.

How long data is kept, and your rights

We don't keep personal information on our own servers. Statistics at TelemetryDeck are kept to show trends over time and can't be linked back to you. Google is responsible for the data its software collects; you can manage it in My Ad Center. We keep emails only as long as we need them to help you.

Depending on where you live, you can ask us what personal information we have about you, and ask us to correct it, delete it, give you a copy, or stop or limit using it. Where we rely on your consent, you can withdraw it at any time. In US states with privacy laws, you can also opt out of the sale or sharing of your personal information in Vectorburst Settings › Privacy choices, and using any of these rights never changes how the game treats you.

In practice, most of this is in your hands: switch off usage statistics in Settings, change your ad choices in Settings or in My Ad Center, and delete the app to delete your progress. For anything else, including a question or request under the GDPR, Canadian law or US state privacy laws, email us and we will reply within 30 days. In Canada, including Quebec, the person in charge of protecting personal information is the owner of Vectorburst, at the same email address. You also have the right to complain to your local data protection authority.

Changes to this policy

If this policy changes, we will update this page and the date at the top.
