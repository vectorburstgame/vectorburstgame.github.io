One tap can clear the whole board.

Vectorburst is a neon chain-reaction puzzle for iPhone. Every node fires a beam when it's hit, and the beam sets off the next node. Find the tap that starts the biggest cascade.

Levels

1,000 boards, each with a par found by a solver. Forks, turners, armored nodes and bombs arrive as you go.

Daily Burst

One new puzzle every day, the same board for everyone. Finish it every day to keep your streak burning.

Burst Run

An endless run with one shared pool of taps. Pick a mod every three boards and build a different run each time.

Plays your way

  • Offline, one thumb, portrait.
  • High-contrast and colour-blind-safe palettes. Every direction is shown by shape, not only by colour.
  • Reduce Motion and Reduce Flashing options, VoiceOver and Dynamic Type.
  • Free to play, with ads and optional in-app purchases. Rewarded ads are always your choice, and Remove Ads turns off the ads between levels. Every purchase shows exactly what you get, and nothing in the game is random for money.
